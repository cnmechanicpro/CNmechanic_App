# Business claims

Authenticated users may submit one open owner, manager, or authorized-representative claim for a published `DISCOVERED` or `UNCLAIMED` organization. The database derives claimant identity from the row policy and permits only the claimant or platform admins to read the claim. A claimant cannot submit for another profile.

A claim does not create organization membership, alter organization state, publish data, or award CN Verified status. Review evidence remains private. Approval/rejection requires an authoritative reviewer and timestamp; no admin mutation endpoint is exposed in Phase 3.
